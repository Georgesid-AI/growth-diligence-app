How your files are processed

Privacy protections and their limits: Email addresses, phone numbers and ID numbers are removed from spreadsheet data and extracted deck text before they are sent to Anthropic. Selected spreadsheet fields containing people's names are withheld, and certain known company and client names are replaced with audit-specific codes. Other labels and figures are sent unchanged. These protections do not identify names written in free text. Names in free text are sent as written. PDF deck images are not masked, so any personal or company information visible on those pages is also sent. Do not rely on automatic masking to remove all sensitive information.

What is sent to the AI: Spreadsheet contents – including sheet names, headers, row labels and figures – are sent after the masking described above. For decks, text from every page containing a figure is sent. For PDF decks, an image of each of those pages is also sent.

Accuracy checks: Extracted figures are checked against their original source and reviewed for reading errors. Figures that do not match are marked "Doesn't match source" and listed for review. These checks do not guarantee that every error will be detected. AI-written commentary uses only calculated results.

Processing and storage: Processing starts automatically when you upload, without a separate confirmation step. Uploaded files are stored for this audit and deleted when the audit is deleted. A file's reading is kept so that uploading the same file again does not re-read it; it is deleted with the last audit that uses it.
