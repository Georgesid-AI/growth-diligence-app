# Claims list changes of the live run

**Status:** built 2026-10-26 (George, from the live run; fixture decks only). Adds to `docs/design/segment-partitions.md` and `docs/design/claim-review-simplified-design.md` (not amended). No architecture change: nothing new reaches a model, the stored page items keep cell ids only, the engine output is unchanged.

**Challenge (rule 4).** Needed: the analyst could not tell why a figure was Medium, what a column meant, or what the app had made of a row, and a legend-chart figure had no way to take its name. Simplest version: one text file, one correction field, one deck-level answer. Deleted first: any model call, any automatic pairing of a legend item with a figure, the card's Keep.

1. **Confidence column** sits after Source and before Status. Under the value, a plain line from the checks the server already names (`confidence.failed`), texts in `frontend/src/lib/claims_list_texts.json` (`reasons`).
2. **Headings**: every heading shows its pop-up on hover or focus and on tap or click; texts in the same file (`columns`).
3. **Made of**: the Type cell reads "Revenue · <segment>" when a segment is set and, for a part of a group, "part of <metric> <period> total" (`claim_groups.made_of`, `made_of` on each candidate of `GET /decks`; registered claims only).
4. **Legend items**: when a figure sits in a chart (`association.Layout`) and its cited label has no cell to replace it, the page offers the short lines near the chart that are no page heading, source line or sentence, chart title, axis tick, date or rotated axis title (at most 8) as a correction of field `segment`: never applied, whatever their number. The click (`POST .../correction`) sets the item as the claim's segment (analyst input), keeps the claim and removes the flag; Undo brings the card back. The card names the total when the waiting figures of one page add up to a stated total within 2% (`attention[].adds_up_to`, `claim_groups.adds_up_to`); Keep is not offered on it. Once every figure of the set has a segment they form a segment partition as in `segment-partitions.md`.
5. **Sales**: a money claim (revenue or sales type, a currency) whose label is "Sales", "Sales by …" or "Total sales" and whose period ends in or before the as-of month asks "Is 'Sales' in this deck revenue or bookings?", one card per deck (`sales_questions` on `GET /decks`, answer `PUT /audits/{id}/decks/{deck_id}/sales`, kept as `sales_choices.<deck_id>` on the audit). Revenue: the claims take the metric Revenue and are tested as revenue. Bookings: Unverified, "bookings not in the data". Unanswered: Unverified, "sales: confirm revenue or bookings".
6. **Wording**: the closed flag word "label mismatch" stays in storage; the screen reads "The app could not confidently link this figure to its label. Which label describes it?".

## Addendum: the metric from the title above a chart (George, 2026-10-26)

A revenue-typed figure whose own text names no metric takes it from the title over its chart (the centred line over the bars) or, when that names none, from the page's heading cells, when the title names exactly one of revenue, ARR, MRR or sales (`association.heading_word`; two words or none leave it unchanged). The page read keeps the closed word as `heading_metric` on the candidate, never the title. Revenue, ARR and MRR become the row's metric; sales follows the deck's Sales answer. The figure's own words and the analyst's metric always win. A pie or other drawing the reader could not draw (page gaps "graphic" or "chart") still offers its legend items, from the whole page. Legend-labelled parts then join the segment partition of the matching total.
