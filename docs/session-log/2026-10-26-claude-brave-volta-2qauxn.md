Deleted (in the note): the annualisation of a slice of a grouped year; the five repeats of the forecast sentence and the "#rank" prefix on the five; the monthly-P&L request when a yearly CAC payback exists; the fallback that lets any date cell correct a period.
Decided: grouping is computed on read (no stored group), flows sum and balances take their last part, 2% as the sum tolerance; a forecast's implied rate is (F ÷ B)^(12 ÷ m) − 1 with a base of full fiscal year → 12 months → none and an observed rate of YoY when 24 months exist, else annualised over the window, Inconclusive under 12 months; a heading supplies a period token by cell id, never its words; chart pairing needs rectangles and rotated chars kept as layout only, never sent; yearly CAC payback uses new ARR from mrr_series when both year ends exist, else new revenue (contract 10). Examples use TestCo and the forecast fixtures only, figures measured on this branch.
Slow or unclear: whether "Contradicted" is the right word for a plan whose implied growth exceeds the observed rate (decision 3 offers Inconclusive instead); the conflict card's loser as a "Not in the data" segment is a guess at what the analyst wants; two of the note's "today" statements (the legend far from the figure, rotated chars read without an upright flag) rest on reading the code, not on a run.
Root cause and rule: the live run showed that one deck figure (a year) was entered four more times as its own parts, because the register has one row per value and no notion of a whole and its parts; rule: a value that another value of the same metric and fiscal year completes within 2% is a slice, never a claim of its own.
