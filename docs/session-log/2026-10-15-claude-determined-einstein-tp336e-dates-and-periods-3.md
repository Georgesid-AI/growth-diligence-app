Deleted: the Inconclusive branch for a growth claim whose compared months are only partly in the data (it compared other months than the claim's, and a figure over the months present was not the claim's growth).
Decided: a growth claim with a compared month outside the data (a base month before the first month, or a Possibly partial month) is Not covered, Unverified, with the months outside the data listed; every chart and table card shows its own period and missing months through one shared PeriodLines component, so the spec's "every computed block" holds on screen; CAGR or "annualised" wins over "since" and "over" in the growth-basis cue rule, and the one older test that expected unknown for "CAGR of 40% since 2020" now expects annualised.
Slow or unclear: nothing; the card test lists every card by test id, so a card added without a period fails the test only if it is added to that list.
Root cause and rule: the partly-covered path had been written for point-in-time figures and reused for growth, whose coverage is that of two months; a coverage rule is tested for each kind of figure it covers, and the three growth cases (YoY base, MoM first month, partial month) are now tests.
Process change to propose: a spec sentence that says "every X" gets a test that enumerates X from the code, not from the test.
