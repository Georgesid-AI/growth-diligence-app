Deleted: the loose "scale word anywhere in a cell" rule of verify.scale_of for neighbour, header and corner cells; the duplicated check-and-store block inside process_deck_pages (now one function, apply_reply).
Decided: a scale from another cell needs a short cell that is a scale mark (alone, or a short label ending in a currency-symbol or bracketed mark, so "Revenue £m" and "Revenue (£'000)" keep working); a number of seven or more digits takes no outside scale; "scale from <cell>" is an informational flag; the figure-level gap (1,000 or more) is rejected as "scale differs from page" unless the page carries a scale mark of its own; stored page replies carry normaliser_version and are checked again on opening the audit with no model call; separators in their own commit.
Slow or unclear: "1 25 000 left as one value" is read as "never merged into 125,000"; the checker still reads it as the figures 1 and 25,000. The fixture job needs the provider key and prompt secrets, so it skipped here.
Root cause and rule: a prose cell holding the word "million" was read as a scale mark for the figure beside it; the rule that prevents it is verify.scale_source (a scale source is a scale mark, never a sentence) with tests in test_scale_source.py.
